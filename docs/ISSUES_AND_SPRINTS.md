# IP and compliance issue spotting, and development sprints

## BNK-028 IP and compliance issue spotting

`spotIssues` takes a founder's structured product profile, in which every flag must be answered true or false, plus dependency licences and the distribution model. Deterministic rules raise questions for qualified counsel in areas such as privacy, money transmission, lending, securities, health data, children's privacy, cross-border operation, automated decisions, biometrics, trademarks and marketing consent. Licence checks flag network copyleft in non-open-source products, copyleft in distributed binaries and unrecognized licences. Results are marked `notLegalAdvice`, refer to qualified counsel, and state that the checklist does not determine legality or compliance.

## BNK-029 development sprints

`SprintBoard` manages one project's sprints: capacity-checked commitments, one active sprint at a time, a work-in-progress limit, a fixed `TODO -> DOING -> DONE` flow, mid-sprint additions tracked separately, a daily burndown against an ideal line, carry-over on close, and velocity computed only from committed, completed points.
